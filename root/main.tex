**Theorem.** The set of natural numbers that equal the sum of the factorials of their decimal digits is exactly $\{1, 2, 145, 40585\}$. That is, defining $S(n) = \sum_{d \,\in\, \text{digits}_{10}(n)} d!$, we have
$$\bigl\{\, n \in \mathbb{N} \mid S(n) = n \,\bigr\} = \{1,\, 2,\, 145,\, 40585\}.$$
Such numbers are known as **factorions** in base 10.
